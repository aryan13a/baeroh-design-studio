About The Studio

Baeroh Design

Based in Raja Park, Jaipur, Baeroh Design Studio crafts bespoke spaces that blend contemporary spatial structure with local artisanal details. Believing that every room holds a memory, our editorial-driven approach creates residential sanctuaries and boutique spaces that feel grounded, sensory, and quietly premium.

How We Work

The Design Journey

I

Discover

We begin with a deep exploration of your habits, daily rituals, and design aspirations. Through soundboards, we establish the narrative foundation of your space.

II

Design

Translating the narrative into precise 3D spatial models and material selections. We curate the custom furnishings and tactile textures that define the rooms.

III

Deliver

Our trusted artisans and contractors bring the designs to life under our rigorous supervision. We handle every detail, culminating in a fully staged spatial reveal.
